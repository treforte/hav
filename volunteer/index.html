    Why Volunteer

    The Best Seat at the Parade Is in It

    Spectators watch the parade. Volunteers make it happen. You'll be right there when a Korean War veteran rides past and the crowd stands up, or when a marching band from a local high school rounds the corner.

    No experience needed. Pick a role that fits you, show up, and our parade committee walks you through the rest. Bring a friend, your family, your coworkers, or your scout troop.

    What to Expect

    Your Parade Day

    Before

    Sign up and get your details

    After you sign up, the parade committee reaches out with your assignment, where to report, and what to bring.

    9 a.m.

    Check in and gear up

    Meet your team lead, pick up your vest or materials, and get a quick walkthrough of your role.

    11 a.m.

    Parade steps off

    Bands, floats, balloons, and veterans head down the route while thousands cheer them on. You're part of it.

    2 p.m.

    Wrap up

    Help close out, then head home knowing you helped Phoenix say thank you to its veterans.
