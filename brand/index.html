Who We Are

A Thank You 30 Years in the Making

In 1997 we held our first parade in Phoenix. We expected a celebration. What we got was something bigger: veterans telling us it was the first time anyone had ever truly thanked them.

That is still the whole point. Honoring America's Veterans brings Arizona together to recognize the service of veterans and the families who stood beside them, through public events, education, and community.

We don't receive government funding for the parade. It happens because people like you decide it should.

Why It Matters to the Veterans We Honor

Pete Haas · Korean War Veteran, Purple Heart Recipient
